One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week: a
boring, specific council mix-up — the car towed for resurfacing, stuck at
a depot till Friday, and the buses don't stop by the house — with a single
word in it that quietly gives the whole thing away.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), one line, 19 words, within the
1–140 character gate. Run it with `python3 main.py`.
